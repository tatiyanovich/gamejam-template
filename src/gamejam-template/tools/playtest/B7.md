# B7 — Danger vignette and flashes

`COPYCAT/Art/Build B3 Gameplay Window` now also builds the two B7 pieces into
`Content/UI/Gameplay/GameplayWindow.prefab`:

- **Danger vignette** — the D8 `DangerVignette` prefab, nested directly under the window root
  (outside the scaled `Layout`, so it stretches to any aspect) and driven by `DangerVignetteView`.
  Orange `#FF9A3D` at alpha `0.35` while the teacher faces the class, red `#E84C4C` pulsing
  `0.35 ↔ 0.6` with a `0.4` second period while she faces the class **and** you hold SPACE
  (`GDD §15`, `ART_BIBLE §7`). Hidden otherwise and the moment the attempt ends.
- **Flash stack** — three `chip_hud` rows above the own desk on the left (`FlashStackView` +
  `FlashRowView`), newest at the bottom, older ones shifted up, each row alive for `0.6` seconds.
  All seven strings of `GDD §13.4`: `ANSWER COPIED!`, `PENCIL SNAP!`, `LOUDER!`, `SHE'S LOOKING!`,
  `DUCK AWAY! 4s`, `DUCK CONFISCATED`, `TOO HOT!`.

`DUCK AWAY!` reads its duration from `DuckConfig.DistractionSeconds` through
`IDuckQuery.GetDistractionSeconds`, so the balance number is not baked into the view.
`TOO HOT!` fires once per attempt — it latches at 80 % suspicion and only resets when the HUD is
reopened for a new attempt.

Two A15 signals were missing and were added: `IExamQuery.OnWrongInput` (reads the existing
`WrongInputEvent`, so the flash fires on **every** mistake, not only when the teacher reacts) and
`IInputQuery.IsLeaning`, a non-reactive query over the Input context that reads the same
`LeanHeld` flag as `AccumulateSuspicionWhileWatchedSystem` and `KeepStaringWhileLeaningSystem`.
