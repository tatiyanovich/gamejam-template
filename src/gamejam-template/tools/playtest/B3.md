# B3 — Gameplay HUD

`COPYCAT/Art/Build B3 Gameplay Window` rebuilds the existing GameplayWindow prefab,
PawTimer bindings, and removes the obsolete static Q and analogue clock hands.
D11 rebuilds preserve PawTimerView and read the updated D2 electronic-clock layout.

The window seeds values on open and subscribes to A15 queries: answers/total, remaining
bell time, suspicion, microphone level/threshold/rearm/cooldown, duck state, neighbour
paw windows, teacher attention, and exam outcome. Closing or disposing unsubscribes.
Clicking the duck or its Q keycap sends the same ThrowDuckRequest as the Q key.

TeacherRemarkEvent preserves the actual cause from teacher systems (microphone alert,
pencil alert, meowing while watched). TeacherQuery delivers it through the existing
ready-event pipeline. Speech lasts 1.2 seconds; Watching lines rotate, final outcomes
win over later remarks. The bell announcement turns the wall clock red at 45 seconds.

The original wall SVG now contains an electronic clock case and dark display.
Digits remain live TMP. Top chips are 60 px tall and clear the window/blackboard frame.
Suspicion occupies (650,388,315,75), left of the teacher and above neighbour desks;
it clears every kitten pose. The speech bubble is (1140,378,260,112), before the right
neighbour's silhouette. The reference composition is 1920×1080.
