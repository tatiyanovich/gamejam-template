# E1 — Mrs. Hisskins animation

`TeacherView` (`Assets/Code/Gameplay/Teacher/Behaviours/TeacherView.cs`) sits on the D11
`Teacher` prefab and turns `ITeacherQuery.OnAttentionChanged` into cut-out motion. It shows the
pose whose name matches the `TeacherAttention` value (`writing`, `turning`, `watching`, `staring`,
`alerted`, `distracted` — the six children built from `art/src/d4/rig.json`) and plays one DOTween
animation per state. All authored offsets, rotations and scales are read from the prefab at `Awake`,
so the view never hardcodes what the rig already says.

| State | Motion |
|---|---|
| **Writing** | chalk arm swings `±8°` around its shoulder pivot, `0.6` second loop (`rig.motion.writing`) |
| **Turning** | head snaps from `−16°` back to `0` in `0.15` s and the pose overshoots from `0.96` scale — the `0.3` second telegraph of `GDD §9` |
| **Watching** | pupils dart `±0.06` units with `0.3…0.6` second pauses, restarting forever (`rig.motion.watching`) |
| **Staring** | leans in from upright to the authored `6°` tilt and `1.05` scale in `0.25` s with `OutBack` |
| **Alerted** | rotation and scale punch, `0.3` s — the sharp `What was that?!` turn |
| **Distracted** | the whole rig walks `4.5` units left over `1.0` s to the spot where the duck lands (`art/src/d7/layout.json`, `x = 600`) and steps back the moment she leaves the state; a `0.06` unit bob sells the steps |

Speech bubbles are **not** part of E1 — B3 already drives them from `ITeacherQuery.OnRemark`
and `OnAttentionChanged` (`GDD §13.3`, D‑76).

`CopycatArtBuilder.BuildTeacher` adds and wires the component, so `COPYCAT/Art/Rebuild D11 Prefabs`
keeps the animation. The pose array is filled from `Enum.GetNames(typeof(TeacherAttention))`, which
means adding a state to the enum without adding a pose to `rig.json` breaks the build loudly.

## World views bound late

`PreloadSessionState` opens the HUD **before** it loads the `Gameplay` scene, so on the very first
entry `GameplayWindow.OnOpen` found neither the teacher nor the paw rings. `BindWorldViews` now runs
again from `OnUpdate` until both are found, which also fixes the B3 ring timers that silently never
appeared during a first attempt (B‑018).

## Repeat verification

Enter Play Mode, press START, wait for the exam, then run `COPYCAT/QA/Test teacher animation`.

six states with a long deadline so the gameplay systems do not take the state back, and it runs at
